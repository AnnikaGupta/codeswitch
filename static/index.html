Does a chatbot talk to you differently once you mention who you are?

Codeswitch runs the same casual request through two chatbots (Claude, GPT) with and without a mentioned ethnicity, gender, or religion that has nothing to do with the task — "give us a group chat name," "write a birthday toast" — and measures what changes: a more informal register, unearned gendered address, a narrow set of stereotyped references, or an outright refusal.
